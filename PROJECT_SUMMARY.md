# SwiftDeliver

**SwiftDeliver** is a last-mile delivery platform (Next.js frontend + Spring Boot backend, Postgres, Redis, OSRM) where clients book, pay and track parcels, couriers deliver with live GPS, and admins supervise — repository `github.com/OMAR-AmD/project_livraison_plateform`. We built it end to end: pricing runs off real OSRM roads and orders are auto-dispatched with OR-Tools VRP at payment capture; a Random Forest scores every GPS fix in-process, and one shared run rule (k=3 consecutive flags, or a single fix ≥ 0.97) drives both the live alert and the dashboard badge; deliveries are sealed with tamper-evident HMAC proofs and closed by GPS or a single-use 6-digit code, now brute-force throttled (5/delivery, 20/courier per 15 min); a local RAG assistant answers over the FAQ and live orders with cancel; and security (JWT/RBAC, rate limiting, logout revocation), Docker, CI and the Render deploy are all in place, verified by 91 backend tests, Playwright suites and a 15-check rehearsal.

What's left follows the roadmap: in **Nov (harden)** the handover throttle moves to shared-store atomic counters (Redis INCR+EXPIRE) for multi-instance, plus a lockout audit log and a mutation-testing pass, along with seal chaining and `verifyChain`; in **Dec (prove on real data)** rotating QR codes and a chain-head anchor, passive collection of 150 labelled runs, retraining v2 with a noisy re-evaluation, per-courier I-JEBA, and a load test + VPS decision; in **Jan (freeze)** the final video and rehearsals. Known gaps: payment is simulated (no real PSP), and the RAG chat is unavailable on the public cloud (it needs a local Ollama), with free-tier sleep, 30-day Postgres and rate-limited OSRM/Nominatim.
