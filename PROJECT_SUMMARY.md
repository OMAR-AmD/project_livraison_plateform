# SwiftDeliver

**SwiftDeliver** is a last-mile delivery platform (Next.js frontend + Spring Boot backend, Postgres, Redis, OSRM) where clients book, pay and track parcels, couriers deliver with live GPS, and admins supervise — repository `github.com/OMAR-AmD/project_livraison_plateform`. We built it end to end: pricing runs off real OSRM roads and orders are auto-dispatched with OR-Tools VRP at payment capture; a Random Forest scores every GPS fix in-process (trained offline, validated on a noisy holdout with synthetic labels — decision-support, not field-proven), and one shared run rule (k=3 consecutive flags, or a single fix ≥ 0.97) drives both the live alert and the dashboard badge; deliveries are sealed with tamper-evident HMAC proofs chained seal-to-seal (each seal commits to its predecessor; deletions leave signed tombstones) and closed by GPS or a single-use 6-digit code, brute-force throttled (5/delivery, 20/courier per 15 min) on shared Redis counters that fail closed if the store is down; a local RAG assistant (Ollama, default 3B model for laptop compatibility, 8B optional) answers over the FAQ and live orders with cancel; and security (JWT/RBAC, rate limiting, logout revocation, no committed secrets — DB_PASSWORD and JWT_SECRET_KEY refuse to boot without real values), Docker, CI (Actions gates, Render deploys on green) and the Render deploy are all in place, verified by 166 backend tests, Playwright suites, a live chain demo and a 15-check rehearsal.

What's left follows the roadmap: **Nov (harden)** is closed — the handover throttle moved to shared Redis counters (atomic INCR+EXPIRE) with a lockout audit log and a PIT mutation pass (53/54 mutants killed), and seals are chained with `verifyChain` (locked head, tombstoned deletions, live 13/13 demo, proof-service PIT 92/92). In **Dec (prove on real data)**: rotating QR codes and a chain-head anchor (which closes the known tail-deletion gap), passive collection of 150 labelled runs, retraining v2 with a noisy re-evaluation, per-courier UEBA, and a load test + VPS decision. In **Jan (freeze)**: the final video and rehearsals. Known gaps: payment is simulated (no real PSP), the RAG chat is unavailable on the public cloud (it needs a local Ollama), demo accounts are public by design, and the free tier sleeps with 30-day Postgres and rate-limited OSRM/Nominatim.
