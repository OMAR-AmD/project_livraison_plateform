FAQ SwiftDeliver:
- Delay policy: If your parcel is more than 10 minutes late, you are entitled to a 10% automatic refund. If the delay exceeds 45 minutes, the delivery is refunded in full.
- Cold chain: Orders containing fresh products are transported in insulated bags maintaining a temperature of 4 degrees Celsius.
- Damaged items: If a product arrives damaged, take a photo and send it to support within 24 hours for a full refund.
- Payment: Online payments are secure. We do not accept cash on delivery.
- Coverage area: We currently deliver across the whole of Casablanca.
- Pricing: The price quoted at checkout is calculated by the server from the real road route between the pickup and the drop-off, not from a straight-line estimate. The fare is 15 MAD base plus 5 MAD per kilometre of road distance, so a short trip costs the 15 MAD minimum. The amount is always computed on the server; the figure shown in the browser cannot be changed by the client. An order is only charged once payment has been captured, and a courier is only assigned after that, so an unpaid order never occupies a courier's capacity.
- Order cancellation: An order can be cancelled free of charge while it is still pending. Once a courier has been assigned and the parcel is in transit, cancellation is no longer possible.
